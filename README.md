# Yellow Candle Journal

A trade journal for supply and demand traders. Log setups with your bias, POI and trigger tags, see equity in R, and find which patterns pay.

- Black background, yellow for wins and bullish, white for losses and bearish.
- Trades are saved in your own browser only. They are never uploaded.
- Use **Backup** to download your trades as a file and **Restore** to load them again.

Not financial advice. CFDs are high risk and most retail accounts lose money.
